Coffee, kept honest.

Daily Grind Co. started as a single roaster in a rented garage and grew into a small team that still tastes every batch before it ships. We buy direct, roast small, and never stop asking where the beans came from.

How it started

In 2016, three friends with one second-hand roaster set out to prove that good coffee didn't need a fancy shopfront — just honest sourcing and a willingness to burn a few batches while learning the craft.

Ten years on, we still roast in small batches, still answer our own emails, and still believe the best cup starts with knowing exactly whose farm it came from.

What we stand for

Direct Trade

We buy straight from growers, at prices agreed together.

Small-Batch Roasting

Roasted in lots under 20kg for a fresher, brighter cup.

Honest Labeling

Farm, altitude, and harvest date on every single bag.

Low-Waste Packaging

Compostable bags and pods, shipped in recycled boxes.
